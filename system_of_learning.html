System of Learning.

First, pick any intriguing topic, no matter its utility. Then, spend 10 hours engaging with it. When you are not engaging with it, enjoy a pleasant or boring human activity, like walking, cooking, or staring at a bird. After those 10 hours, write a blog post about the experience. Talk about what you learned, what you created, and how it all made you feel. Not much is required; even 100 words will do. And after this exercise, you are free to repeat it with an entirely new interest, or the same if you please.
